The Workbook

The Book That Meets You Where You Are

This workbook is organized around the four major milestones of a modern therapy career. It is designed so you can jump in wherever you are in your journey. Each stage builds on the next — by completing this workbook, you will be able to move forward with clarity and confidence.

Stage 1 — Graduation
  • · Build your foundation
  • · Choose your license type
  • · Find a supervisor
  • · Study for the exam
  • · Gather state requirements
Stage 2 — Licensure
  • · Complete all hours
  • · Pass your licensing exam
  • · Apply for full licensure
  • · Plan your business structure
  • · CAQH and NPI setup
Stage 3 — Launch
  • · Register your LLC
  • · Get your EIN
  • · Get liability insurance
  • · Choose your EHR
  • · Build marketing profiles
Stage 4 — Expansion
  • · Scale your caseload
  • · Credential with insurers
  • · Build referral networks
  • · Create a website & SEO
  • · Self-care & burnout prevention

How to get the most out of this workbook: Use it in order OR jump to your current stage. Complete the exercises as you go — written goals dramatically increase follow-through. Return to it as needed; revisit every few months as your practice evolves. Give yourself grace — this is a process, not a test.

About Morgan

Hi, I'm Morgan.

LPC, LMHC, NCC, QS & Founder of Omata LLC

LPC · LMHC · NCC · QS

Hello, I’m Morgan Ajello, LPC, LMHC, NCC, QS. I am a licensed mental health therapist, a nationally board-certified counselor, and the founder of Omata LLC. With a decade’s worth of clinical experience and training licensed across multiple states and recognized as a Qualified Supervisor through NBCC, I bring both the credentials and the lived experience to back what I teach to future clinicians.

After the rewarding experience of being invited to teach at my alma mater, Johnson & Wales University, I was once again reminded of the missing link between graduation and licensure that created such confusion. My journey was shaped by the complexities of licensure requirements, a lack of education on running a business, and moments questioning why I even entered this field in the first place. Building a practice from the ground up without a clue, without a mentor, and without the resources I wished had existed was a difficult learning experience.

Every challenge you may face and every question you may have is what I aim to answer in this first-of-its-kind fillable workbook to avoid that unnecessary difficulty. In this workbook, you’ll find guidance spanning across the licensure process, launching your own private practice, growing a caseload, and other important information such as insurance credentialing.

The core mission I’ve carried since college is to help all “One Mind At A Time.” To grow boldly, lead with integrity, and never lose sight of the clients and clinicians at the heart of my work.

The Origin Story

Why I Started Omata LLC

When I graduated with my M.S. in Counseling Psychology, I felt like I had been handed a diploma and pointed toward a cliff edge. There was no roadmap for what came next — no one told me how to find a supervisor, track my hours, form an LLC, set fees, or handle insurance credentialing. I figured it out over a decade, one hard lesson at a time.

Omata LLC — One Mind At A Time — exists because that experience should not be the norm. I built the workbook and the CEU course I wish I'd had. I want every new clinician to walk into their career with clarity, confidence, and a plan.

Course Description

About This Course

Ready to build a private practice that is sustainable, ethically grounded, and professionally fulfilling? Helping All One Mind At A Time is a comprehensive, self-paced home study course designed by Morgan Ajello, LPC, LMHC, NCC, QS — a licensed therapist and practice owner with a decade's worth of experience.

This course walks you through every stage of building a private practice: from licensure planning and business formation to marketing, insurance credentialing, fee-setting, ethical templates, and clinician self-care. Whether you're still completing your supervised hours or already seeing clients, this course meets you where you are.

Learning Objectives

Upon Completion, Participants Will Be Able To:

1

Identify the key milestones in the journey from graduate school to full licensure and private practice ownership.

2

Describe the components of a well-documented clinical supervision agreement and how to establish an effective supervisory relationship.

3

Explain the legal and ethical considerations involved in forming a private practice LLC, including EIN registration and liability insurance.

4

Apply a structured methodology for setting competitive yet sustainable fees in private practice, including sliding scale considerations.

5

Outline the steps for credentialing with insurance panels, including CAQH, EFT setup, and panel-specific credentialing timelines.

6

Develop a targeted referral outreach strategy using professional platforms, peer networks, and community partnerships.

7

Identify the documentation required for ethical private practice, including intake forms, consent forms, and policies for telehealth.

8

Evaluate personal risk factors for burnout and apply a self-care framework designed specifically for licensed mental health clinicians.

9

Construct a personalized Private Practice Launch Plan incorporating all stages: Graduation, Licensure, Launch, and Expansion.
